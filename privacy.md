## Google Analytics

This site uses Google Analytics 4 to understand which pages are visited, how long visitors actively engage with them, and how visitors arrive at the site. Analytics runs only after you choose **Accept analytics** in the consent notice.

The analytics reports may include page titles and paths, referral or campaign sources, engagement measurements, and browser or device information. Google processes analytics data under its [Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

This site does not send names, email addresses, or profession information to Google Analytics. Google Signals, advertising storage, and advertising personalization are not enabled by this setup.
